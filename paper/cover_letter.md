Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "SWPL: A Skew–Phase Law for Data Structure Selection" for consideration for publication in Knowledge-Based Systems.

**Why KBS?** Knowledge-Based Systems focuses on intelligent systems that incorporate domain knowledge into decision-making. SWPL provides a provable, knowledge-driven rule for data structure selection — replacing empirical heuristics with a mathematical law grounded in the Riemann zeta function's convergence properties. This aligns directly with KBS's scope of bridging formal knowledge and practical system design.

**Contributions:** This paper makes three contributions:
1. We prove a lower bound on expected search cost under Zipf-skewed workloads, showing a sharp phase transition at α=1;
2. We construct the ζ-Trie, a probability-mass-partitioned data structure that matches the bound and is therefore asymptotically optimal;
3. We provide extensive experimental validation and an open-source implementation (pip install swpl).

**Reproducibility:** All code and experimental data are publicly available at https://github.com/ZhangYangyi03/swpl and on PyPI (pip install swpl). The proof is self-contained in the manuscript.

I confirm that this work is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere.
